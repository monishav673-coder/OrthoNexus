# OrthoNexus
OrthoNexus AI is an AI-powered orthopedic intelligence platform designed to support knee analysis, personalized digital knee modeling, explainable AI insights, implant-fit simulation, and orthopedic research visualization.

🦴 See the knee. Understand the future.

🚀 Project Overview

OrthoNexus AI brings multiple intelligent orthopedic features together into a single workspace. The platform is designed as a research and educational prototype demonstrating how AI-assisted tools can support knee assessment and orthopedic decision-support workflows.

The system provides an interactive dashboard where users can explore knee analysis, KneeTwin AI, Explainable AI, Implant Simulator, and Research Intelligence modules.

✨ Key Features
🩻 AI Knee Analysis
Upload knee X-ray images
Preview uploaded images
Analyze sample/demo cases
Display AI-generated assessment results
Show confidence and risk indicators

Creates a personalized digital representation of a knee

🔍 Explainable AI

Visualizes analysis indicators

🦿 Implant Fit Simulator

📊 Research Intelligence

⚠️ Disclaimer

OrthoNexus AI is a research and educational prototype.

The platform is not intended to provide medical diagnosis, treatment recommendations, or clinical decisions. Any analysis, risk score, confidence value, or implant simulation shown in the demo should be considered illustrative/sample output only and must not be used for real patient care.
